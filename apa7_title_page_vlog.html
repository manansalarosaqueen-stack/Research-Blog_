Episode 01 · Anatomy of the Page

Key Elements of an APA 7 Title Page

APA 7 simplified title pages for student papers by removing the running head (unless your school specifically requests it).

01 / TOP RIGHT

Page Number

Positioned flush right in the header section starting on page 1. Use standard Arabic numerals (1).

02 / CENTERED & BOLD

Paper Title

Placed 3–4 lines down from the top margin. Written in Title Case, centered, bolded, and double-spaced.

03 / AUTHOR NAME

Author Byline

Positioned 1–2 blank lines after the title. Include First Name, Middle Initial(s), and Last Name without titles (e.g., omit "Dr." or "Mr.").

04 / AFFILIATION

Institutional Affiliation

States the specific department/college and university name (e.g., Department of Psychology, University of Example).

05 / COURSE INFO

Course Number & Name

Includes the course code and full title (e.g., PSY 499: Senior Research Seminar & Thesis).

06 / INSTRUCTOR

Instructor Name

Includes the instructor’s or adviser’s full title and name (e.g., Dr. Maria Santos) on its own line.

07 / SUBMISSION DATE

Due Date

Written in standard date format (e.g., October 15, 2026) reflecting the submission date.

08 / PROFESSIONAL OPTION

Author Note (Optional)

Primarily for professional papers or publications. Placed at the bottom half to list ORCID iDs, disclosures, or contact info.

09 / INSTITUTIONAL VARIATIONS

School Guidelines

Some universities require extra lines like panelist approval dates or degree specifications. Always check your school handbook!

Episode 03 · Pro-Tips

Title Page Quick Checklist

✅ Title Length: Keep the title focused, concise, and clear (typically 10–12 words).
✅ No Running Head: APA 7 student papers do not need a running head unless specified by your instructor.
✅ Formatting: Double-space all lines on the title page, just like the rest of your paper.
✅ Font Choice: Use standard APA fonts (e.g., 12 pt Times New Roman, 11 pt Arial, or 11 pt Calibri).
✅ Capitalization: Use Title Case for the paper title (capitalize major words and words with 4+ letters).
✅ Margins: Maintain 1-inch (2.54 cm) margins on all four sides of the page.
