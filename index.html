Quick Reference · Formatting Checklist

Essential APA 7 Rules

✅ Font Standards: Use 12 pt Times New Roman, 11 pt Arial, or 11 pt Calibri throughout.
✅ Margins & Spacing: Maintain 1-inch margins on all sides and double-space all body text.
✅ Header Page Numbers: Place page numbers flush right in the top header starting on page 1.
✅ Headings Hierarchy: Apply clear APA 7 bolded and italicized heading levels (Levels 1–5).
