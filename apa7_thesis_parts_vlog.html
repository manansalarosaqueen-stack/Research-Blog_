Chapter 01 · The main content

The parts you’ll usually see in a thesis

Schools may use different institutional formats. Always follow your adviser’s or department’s prescribed template when it differs from this general guide.

01 / FRONT MATTER

Title Page

Contains the thesis title, author, institutional affiliation, degree or course, and submission date. Follow your school’s required arrangement.

02 / FRONT MATTER

Abstract

A brief overview of the study: problem, method, major findings, and conclusion. APA papers commonly use a concise abstract, often around 150–250 words.

03 / FRONT MATTER

Acknowledgments

Recognizes people or institutions that supported the research. This is often required by schools but is not a universal APA manuscript element.

04 / FRONT MATTER

Table of Contents

Lists chapters, headings, and page numbers. Include lists of tables and figures if required by your institution.

05 / CHAPTER 1

Introduction

Introduces the topic, background, research gap, purpose, research questions, significance, scope, and important terms.

06 / CHAPTER 2

Review of Related Literature

Synthesizes theories, previous studies, concepts, and evidence related to your research problem. Cite sources using APA style.

07 / CHAPTER 3

Methodology

Explains the research design, participants or data sources, instruments, procedures, ethical considerations, and data-analysis plan.

08 / CHAPTER 4

Results / Findings

Presents what the data showed through text, tables, and figures. Avoid repeating every value already visible in a table.

09 / CHAPTER 5

Discussion & Conclusion

Interprets findings, connects them to literature, explains implications, states limitations, and gives recommendations when appropriate.

10 / BACK MATTER

References

Lists every source cited in the thesis. Use APA 7 author-date citations and a matching reference entry for each cited work.

11 / BACK MATTER

Appendices

Includes supporting materials such as questionnaires, interview guides, consent forms, letters, raw summaries, or extra tables.

12 / SCHOOL REQUIREMENT

Other Pages

May include approval sheets, certification, curriculum vitae, plagiarism report, ethics clearance, or biographical information depending on the school.

Chapter 02 · The usual sequence

Think of your thesis as a vlog playlist

Each part has a job. Together, they tell the story of your research.

1

Hook the audience — Title & Abstract

Tell readers what your study is about and give them a quick preview of the whole research.

2

Set the context — Introduction & RRL

Explain why the problem matters and what previous researchers have already discovered.

3

Show your process — Methodology

Make the research process clear enough for readers to understand and evaluate.

4

Reveal the content — Results

Present the evidence gathered from your participants, documents, tests, observations, or other data.

5

Wrap up — Discussion, Conclusion & References

Explain what the results mean, answer the research questions, and credit every source used.

Chapter 03 · APA 7 essentials

Before you hit “submit”

✅ Use consistent headings.
Apply the required APA heading levels and your school’s chapter format.
✅ Cite borrowed ideas.
Use author-date in-text citations and include matching reference entries.
✅ Format tables and figures.
Number them, give descriptive titles, and add notes when needed.
✅ Keep the language academic.
Be clear, precise, objective, and consistent in terminology.
✅ Check the reference list.
Use a hanging indent, alphabetical order, and correct DOI/URL formatting when applicable.
✅ Follow institutional rules.
Margins, front matter, chapter labels, signatures, and page order may be school-specific.
